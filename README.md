# ☕ Cafe & City Catch (Beyond Hello, World! HD Edition)

A high-definition 2D arcade browser game built with **HTML5 Canvas**, **Web Audio API**, and **Python (FastAPI / SQLite)**.

Featuring:
- 🐷 **Pippin the Barista Pig**: Wearing his green coffee bean apron and holding hot coffee, catching fresh cafe pastries and espresso!
- 🕊️ **Percy the City Pigeon**: The cheerful city pigeon walking on skinny legs, catching street bakery slices, pretzels, and fries!

---

## 🎮 Characters & Unique Mechanics

### 1. 🐷 Pippin (The Barista Pig)
- **Theme**: Cozy Cafe & Bakery
- **Favorite Treats**: ☕ Espresso Cup (+10), 🥐 Butter Croissant (+15), 🍩 Glazed Donut (+20), 🧋 Boba Milk Tea (+35), ✨☕ Golden Coffee Bean (+50)
- **Active Skill (`SHIFT` / `⚡ SKILL`)**: **Espresso Rush** — Steam-powered supersonic dash with invulnerability frames.
- **Super Power (`SPACE` / `🌟 SUPER`)**: **Cafe Frenzy** — Sweet aroma magnet draws all pastries and coffee from the sky straight into Pippin's cup for 6 seconds!

### 2. 🕊️ Percy (The City Pigeon)
- **Theme**: Urban Bakery & Street Food
- **Favorite Treats**: 🍞 Bread Slice (+10), 🍟 Crispy Fries (+15), 🥨 Warm Pretzel (+20), 🍕 Pizza Slice (+35), 🪙 Shiny Gold Coin (+50)
- **Active Skill (`SHIFT` / `⚡ SKILL`)**: **Wing Flap Gust** — Sonic wind gust that blows away all falling bombs and trash with feather sparks!
- **Super Power (`SPACE` / `🌟 SUPER`)**: **Feather Storm Zoomies** — Percy enters hyper peck mode: crushes bombs for bonus points and summons a shower of street treats!

---

## 🌟 Modern Visuals & Features

- **Crystal-Clear Vector Rendering**: High-definition vector pastries, food, and items with smooth anti-aliased curves.
- **Juicy Character Animation**: Dynamic walk-bobbing, directional tilting, squash-and-stretch on catch, and speed dash trails.
- **Dynamic Atmosphere**: Seamless time-of-day progression from **Sunny Morning ☀️** ➔ **Warm Sunset 🌅** ➔ **Starry Midnight with Moon 🌙**.
- **Webinar Leaderboard**: Real-time score submission with anti-cheat session verification and Top 10 rankings.

---

## 🕹️ Controls

- **Move Left / Right**: `A` / `D` or `◄` / `►` (or on-screen touch arrows)
- **Active Skill**: `SHIFT` or `⚡ SKILL`
- **Super Move**: `SPACE` / `E` or `🌟 SUPER`
- **Pause / Resume**: `P` key or pause button
- **Sound Toggle**: `🔊` button at top-right
